# Judge invocation and reproducibility

Each of three Codex evaluators was launched with **no conversation history**. All inherited the configured runtime model. The precise backend model identifier and temperature were not exposed; do not substitute an invented version. The full packet hashes are in `packet_manifest.json`. Judgments are stochastic artifacts retained verbatim with per-item rationales; rerunning another judge may differ.

The independent signal is direct text-and-evidence judgment, not MiniLM vectors, centroid similarity, merge objectives or cluster labels supplied to the coherence judge. Configuration identities were hidden in `PRIVATE_identity_key.json` and only decoded after judgments were saved. Coherence and faithfulness judges were restricted to their respective packet file; the retrieval judge to its packet file. They were instructed not to read other project files or browse the web. The packet builder and aggregator are code, but ratings were individually produced by the LLM judges, not generated by a string-matching script.

## Coherence instructions

Read every packet independently. Use 1=incoherent/mere names or broad domain only, 2=mixed subthemes, 3=mostly recognizable specific scientific theme with exceptions, 4=clear specific scientific concept. Materials science alone, type identity and name similarity are insufficient. Judge sampled evidence, not unseen members. For every packet record rating, topic-specific rationale and uncertainty. Do not inflate results. No model scores or identity key may be inspected.

## Faithfulness instructions

Judge each unchanged label/gloss against member text and available relations. Choose accurate_informative, supported_but_vague, unsupported_or_wrong or insufficient_evidence. Examples do not assert universal membership properties. Consider misleading thematic implication and informativeness, not copy accuracy alone. Record rationale, misleading_theme flag and evidence limitations. The source is a structured extraction, not full papers or external scientific verification.

## Retrieval instructions

Use only returned evidence, never facts available solely in the rubric. For Type A, evaluate every expected method with direct_node_hit and mention_hit separately; only a method/dataset node can be a direct hit. Unambiguous aliases require rationale. Rate every expected method claim by target and zero-based index. For Type B, rate every required claim by its rubric ID. Statuses are full/partial/absent/contradicted. Return exact supporting evidence IDs. For all questions evaluate every required citation using attached provenance titles or explicit returned citation strings, not remembered author/title associations. A supplied title-to-citation rubric mapping may disambiguate a provenance title. Unknown mappings remain uncovered. Honor empirical/asserted-only caveats. No new retrieval, generation or ranking changes. Include absent criteria to preserve denominators.

## Controller checks

Validate packet completeness, legal categories, exact expected-target sets and that support IDs actually occur in the corresponding returned list. Do not change judge ratings to improve scores. Any unresolved output-format issue may be corrected without exposing identities. Human spot-checks are a separate file; blank cells stay pending. The initial developer review remains separate from these new checks.
